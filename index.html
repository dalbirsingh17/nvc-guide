This will walk you through the four NVC steps: what you observed, what you're feeling, the need underneath it, and a request you can actually make.

Step 1 of 4 · Observation

What actually happened?

Just the facts, as a camera or microphone would record them. Specific to this time and place.

"When I hear you say / see you…
Watch for evaluation. Words like always, never, rude, lazy, ignoring me are judgments, not observations. If a video camera couldn't have captured it, rewrite it.
Step 2 of 4 · Feeling

What's alive in you right now?

Feelings are body signals about your needs. Pick one or a few that ring true.

It feels like they did something to me ("disrespected", "ignored"…)

These words are evaluations of the other person, not feelings. Tap the one that fits and I'll translate it into feelings and needs.

Step 3 of 4 · Need

What do you need?

Needs are universal — they name what would sustain or enrich life here, without naming any person or any specific action.

Nothing selected yet
Need vs. strategy. "Respect" is a need. "You texting me back within an hour" is a strategy. Stay at the need level here — the strategy comes next.
Step 4 of 4 · Request

Ask for something doable.

A request is concrete, positive (what you DO want), and possible right now. Not a demand, not a vague wish.

Request vs. demand. If "no" would earn them punishment, it's a demand. Be ready to hear no and stay in the conversation.
